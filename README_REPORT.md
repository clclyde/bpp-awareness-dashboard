# BPP brand awareness review deck

`report_deck.py` builds an 8-slide .pptx from the same numbers as the dashboard (`data.py`), in the Project Swarm look (white, navy block, yellow title word, red bar, Montserrat). Native editable charts and tables; opens in PowerPoint and Google Slides (File > Import slides). Install Montserrat for PowerPoint; Google Slides has it.

```bash
pip install -r requirements-report.txt
python report_deck.py --month 2026-09            # local snapshot (data/bpp_snapshot.json)
python report_deck.py --month 2026-09 --live     # Supabase, using .streamlit/secrets.toml or PULSE_DB_* variables
python report_deck.py --month 2026-09 --internal # adds the team-only data check (yellow months, note on the trend slide)
python report_deck.py --halves                   # half vs half review, the KPI Cards tab as slides (default: 6 months from Oct 2025)
python report_deck.py --halves --start 2025-10 --n 6 --live
```
Half vs half output: `out/BPP_Awareness_Halves_<start>_<n>m_<layout>.pptx`, 2 slides, both titled "Platform Distribution Level Up", in the look of the "Share-Led Content Focus" deck (cream page, dark ink, deep green for results that improved, burnt orange for results that got worse, DM Sans, white rounded cards, dark note bar; install DM Sans for PowerPoint, Google Slides has it). Slide 1 is how far the content travelled and how many people followed (average reach per month first, then Facebook followers, reach per Facebook post, Facebook post views (impressions), new Facebook follows, TikTok followers, TikTok reach, TikTok engagements, TikTok video views, total reach, ad impressions, unique reach per month, reach and impressions per ₱1,000, plus a note on unfollows and boosted posts); slide 2 is what each peso delivered (new follows per ₱1,000, engagements per ₱1,000, cost per engagement, ad post engagements, CPM, ad engagement rate, click-through rate, cost per click, cost per 1,000 reached, spend, true frequency, posts, videos, Facebook engagement rate). The Facebook followers and TikTok followers, reach and engagement cards are whole-period figures supplied by the team (`pulse.fb_page_metrics` rows `fb_followers`, `tt_followers`, `tt_reach`, `tt_engagement`), shown only when they were stored for exactly the two periods compared; their source is still to be confirmed. `--layout cards` (default): every figure is a card, three key cards with monthly sparklines and smaller cards below, in the order number now, filled change pill, previous number. `--layout halves`: like the dashboard's first tab, the first period in a panel on the left (numbers with a Baseline pill) and the next period on the right (numbers with the change pill), every metric level across both. `--layout table`: three key cards and the other figures as table rows. The cards come from `data.half_cards`, the same list the dashboard's KPI Cards tab uses; a card the layout does not list is added to slide 2. The key cards are the first three that exist of the candidates in `HalvesDeck.LAYOUT` (so the deck still builds when the follows are not loaded); the new follows per ₱1,000 card gets its monthly sparkline from the deck, not from `data.half_cards`. The account-level data adds unique reach per month, true frequency, click-through rate and cost per click, and the Business Suite follows add new Facebook follows and new follows per ₱1,000 (before unfollows; the second divides all new follows, organic and paid, by Facebook ad spend); the deck shows true frequency in place of Average frequency, and the TikTok analytics engagement in place of the engagement per view of the videos (`HalvesDeck.SUPERSEDED`). Speaker notes explain how to read the slides. The monthly sparklines are drawn as plain shapes (a grouped line and dots per sparkline), not as charts, because Google Slides shows the axes of an imported chart even when they are hidden. The monthly deck keeps the Project Swarm look and its native charts.

Monthly output: `out/BPP_Awareness_YYYY-MM.pptx` (git-ignored: real client numbers). Without `--month` it uses the last full month.

Slides: 1 cover, 2 headline and three key figures, 3 paid awareness (5 KPIs + key takeaway), 4 awareness trend (4 charts, 12 months), 5 paid campaigns, 6 Facebook page, 7 TikTok, 8 what to look at next + planner's notes box + definitions.

Rules: the text is rule-based (no LLM); a person checks the numbers against Meta and TikTok and writes the why and what next (Playbook 3.1, 4.9), so the closing slide leaves a box for the planner. The default build is the client version (no data-quality check).
