# BPP brand awareness dashboard: project context

Own project (private repo `clclyde/bpp-awareness-dashboard`, separate from the PediaWell dashboard). Streamlit 1.66.0 prototype for BPP (Budol-Proof Pinoy: Facebook page and TikTok), requested by Sir Joven.

- Design (editorial, redone 2026-10-20): the page opens with the finding as a headline sentence, then big figures with sparklines (native `st.metric`, no card boxes), then evidence. Colours are the Project Swarm deck palette (white, navy #073763, blue #0B3691, yellow #F1C232, red #D10000, text #3F3F3F); fonts Montserrat (body) and Source Serif 4 (headings), all set in `.streamlit/config.toml`. Custom HTML: a plain yellow rule at the top, a red bar at the bottom, and the KPI Cards tab (added 2026-10-21 at Sir Joven's request: the half vs half comparison as cards, read in his order: the number now, a filled change pill green = better / red = worse / grey = within 2%, then the previous number and the absolute change; key KPIs add a monthly sparkline with hover, first period grey and second blue; own period selector `kc_n` / `kc_start`, default 6 months from Oct 2025; groups ordered to make the renewal case, strongest first: Audience Growth, More From Every Peso Spent, Spend and Activity (every figure stays on the page, only the order changes); cards in a row have equal height; CTR and cost per click appear only when both periods have clicks, i.e. from Mar 2026 or after the monthly campaign reports are loaded). Tabs: First vs second half (default view, requested by Sir Joven 2026-10-20: two equal periods side by side with growth and cost, own selector of period length and start month, default 6 months per period, first period starting Oct 2025: Oct 2025 to Mar 2026 vs Apr to Sep 2026 (chosen 2026-10-21; the start month stays selectable); sections ordered strongest first for the renewal case (2026-10-21): Facebook Page and TikTok (reach per post, post views, TikTok views, posts, engagement rate), Cost Efficiency (cost per engagement first), Paid Awareness (reach, ad engagement rate, cost per 1,000 reached, spend, frequency); TikTok is only compared when its data covers the first period; no video view-through here, the exports have no ThruPlays before Mar 2026), Overview, Awareness trend, Content, Channel Diagnostics. Channel Diagnostics (added 2026-10-21, mirrors the PediaWell tab of the same name; two equal periods with its own selector, 6 months per period and the first period starting Oct 2025 by default, each channel on two adjacent rows: first period = Baseline, second period = status vs the first): paid channels table (Facebook ads full metrics, TikTok ads spend only), organic channels table (Facebook page, TikTok), Facebook ads by campaign type. Status = change in cost per mille (paid) or engagement rate / engagement per view (organic) from the first period to the second, with the PediaWell thresholds (Improved at 5% better or more, Steady within 5%, Slightly weaker up to 15% worse, Weaker beyond; proposed, to be calibrated). A campaign type with under 2% of the spend gets no status. TikTok ad spend (`ref_tt`: snapshot key `tiktok_ad_spend`, Supabase `pulse.ad_spend_monthly` client_code BPP-TT, source_tab 'SSOT TT tabs' for Aug and Oct 2025, 'Ad Spend Report 2025' for Sep, Nov and Dec 2025 (Sep: the SEPT - TT tab has boost rows without a video that the billing does not include), 'Campaign Spend Monitoring' for 2026) is shown only when every month of a period has a figure. The first tab has a Cost Efficiency section (added 2026-10-21 at Sir Joven's request: unit costs should go down and what each PHP 1,000 buys should go up): cost per mille, per 1,000 reached, per engagement, plus per click and per ThruPlay only when both periods are from Mar 2026 onward (earlier exports have no clicks or ThruPlays), and a by-campaign-type table, because the blended costs also move with the campaign mix (`data.unit_costs`, `data.unit_costs_by_objective`). Follows Streamlit's own design guidance: theme in config.toml, native metrics/dataframes, no CSS unless asked. Needs Streamlit 1.66 (bordered/sparkline metrics, `delta_description`, theme fonts and chart palettes).
- Data: Supabase `pulse.social_ads_monthly`, `fb_posts`, `tiktok_videos`, `fb_page_metrics` and BPP rows of `ad_spend_monthly`, read with the read-only `pulse_reader_bpp` login (secrets or `PULSE_DB_*` env vars). Meta ads (added 2026-10-29): `data.SQL['ads_api']` reads `pulse.api_meta_ads_monthly` (campaign rows) and `pulse.api_meta_account_monthly` (account totals, unique reach), loaded weekly by the n8n workflow `pulse32_meta_ads` from the Marketing API; `social_ads_monthly` only supplies the months before the API tables start (Aug and Sep 2025). The reader needs `supabase/28_bpp_reader_api_grants.sql`; without it the app silently falls back to `social_ads_monthly` (sidebar source says 'Supabase (live, Meta API)' only when the API tables are read). Differences to the SSOT exports: API campaign rows carry clicks, ThruPlays, reactions, comments, shares and saves for every month (the old exports lacked them before Mar 2026, so CTR, cost per click and cost per ThruPlay cards now show for the Oct 2025 halves), Mar 2026 reach is the campaign-level sum (the SSOT had ad-level rows), Sep 2026 spend 19,458.59 and campaign reach 1,014,556 (was 19,458.54 and 1,014,541: late attribution); account reach is identical. Facebook posts (added 2026-10-29): `data.SQL['posts_api']` reads `pulse.api_fb_posts` (n8n workflow `pulse32_facebook_posts`, Graph API `/published_posts` of page 760988917093781, lifetime numbers, last 60 days re-pulled weekly); `fb_posts` is the fallback. Mapping: engagements = reactions + comments + shares (as in the Business Suite exports), reach = Meta's unique media viewers (`post_total_media_view_unique`, within about 6% of the Business Suite reach per post, so it is a different definition), published converted to Manila time, type Photos/Videos from status_type, no duration. Effect on the halves (Oct 2025 start): post views 949,079 to 1,014,523 and 1,221,699 to 1,285,532, reach per post 10,859 to 11,520 and 14,950 to 16,111, engagement rate 4.11% to 4.13% and 2.10% to 1.98%; Feb and May 2026 post views are about 19% higher than the old exports (those were taken early). Sources of both are shown only in the internal view. Page follows (added 2026-10-29): `data.SQL['page_api']` sums the daily `pulse.api_fb_page_daily` (n8n workflow `pulse32_facebook_posts`, page insights `page_daily_follows_unique`; also stores `page_follows` total followers and `page_daily_unfollows_unique` per day) into `follows_month` for complete months from Aug 2025, replacing the Business Suite CSV values; they are identical for every month Sep 2025 to Sep 2026 (336, 34, 733 ... 167), and the API adds Aug 2025 (868). The whole-period rows (followers_lifetime, net_follows, unfollows, fb_followers, tt_*) stay as supplied: API followers are 5,584 on 31 Mar 2026 and 12,724 on 30 Sep 2026 against the supplied 5,092 and 12,769 (dates or definition differ, to confirm with the team), and API unfollows Sep 2025 to Sep 2026 sum to 1,376 against 1,382 in the screenshot. Monthly unfollows are now available (net new followers per month could be added). Followers switched to the API 2026-10-30 (Clyde's decision): `data.platform_period` takes Facebook followers from the daily `page_follows` on the last day of the period (any period whose last day is in the data, so 3-month periods now show it too; none when the period ends in the current partial month), instead of the supplied 5,092 and 12,769, which become 5,584 and 12,724 for the default halves; the whole-period caption figures (net followers, unfollows, followers in total, label from `S['page_label']`) are computed from the same daily series (11,168 net, 1,376 unfollows, 12,724 followers for Sep 2025 to Sep 2026, against the Audience screenshot's 10,664, 1,382 and 12,721). `S['fb_followers_api']` switches the captions in app.py and report_deck.py; without the daily table (snapshot mode or no grant) the supplied figures and old captions stay. TikTok followers, reach and engagements are still the supplied figures. Local fallback: git-ignored `data/bpp_snapshot.json`. Source of the data: BPP Content SSOT sheet (Ads Manager exports, Facebook post tabs + YTD Meta, YTD TikTok + the 2025 TT tabs). Refresh is manual: rebuild the snapshot, run `supabase/loaders/gen_22_bpp_seed.py` (parent project, git-ignored), run the seed in Supabase.
- Definitions follow the deck: reach is the sum of campaign reach rows (overstates unique reach), monthly not weekly, no target audience yet (card pending); frequency = impressions / reach; cost per 1,000 reached = spend / reach x 1,000 (reach campaigns only in comparisons); engagement rate = (reactions + comments + shares) / reach; TikTok has views only. Aug and Sep 2025 figures in the SSOT "Tables" tab are 2025, not targets.
- TikTok has no videos in the SSOT for Oct to Dec 2025: those posts were added from TikTok Studio Posts-list screenshots (lifetime views/likes/comments, no shares, no links; a list value shown as "30K" is rounded). Any month that still has no TikTok rows is treated as missing data, not zero: the comparison tab shows the partial total labelled "(partial)" with the months it covers, and no change percentage. The explanatory notes about the missing months (comparison tab and Overview) are internal-view only; the client sees just the "(partial)" label and the months it covers.
- Data fixes 2026-10-21: AUG - AM contains its export twice (second block column-shifted) and OCT - AM row 27 repeats row 7; both duplicates are removed from the snapshot/seed (215 ad rows). Facebook post tabs include boosted posts (most views are boosted), so never label them organic. The Ad Spend Report's BPP-TT line mixes ex-VAT and VAT-inclusive amounts; the Campaign Spend Monitoring sheet has the consistent TikTok spend for 2026; Nov and Dec 2025 TikTok spend come from the Summary 2025 tab of the Ad Spend Report 2025 (source_tab 'Ad Spend Report 2025'). Full audit: parent project supabase/BPP_SOURCE_AUDIT.md.
- Known data gaps: No month is flagged incomplete any more (Feb and May 2026 were re-exported on 2026-10-22). The Ad Spend Report's April figure is VAT-inclusive, so the app uses the ex-VAT amount from Campaign Spend Monitoring instead (snapshot key `ad_spend_fb_csm`, Supabase `ad_spend_monthly` BPP-FB rows with source_tab 'Campaign Spend Monitoring'); March 2026 is exported per ad; the JUL - Meta tab header is broken (handled).
- Account-level data (added 2026-10-22 from the `Ad Account - Oct 2025 to Sept 2026.xlsx` that Mich uploads to the Meta Drive folder, Meta Ads Manager account level by month): 12 rows in `social_ads_monthly` with level `account` (Meta's own totals of spend, impressions, unique reach and clicks (all)). `data._prepare` splits them into `S["acct"]`; they are never summed with the campaign rows. They feed four cards in `data.half_cards` (unique people reached per month, true frequency, click-through rate, cost per click; each needs an account row for every month of both periods) and confirmed that the Feb and May 2026 campaign tabs are short and that Apr is complete. Oct 2025 was corrected: one boosting campaign running Oct 1 to Nov 7 sat in both the OCT and NOV tabs (the November part was subtracted from the October row). Seed 22 widens the level check to allow `account`. Feb and May 2026 were re-exported by Mich on 2026-10-22 (campaign level, Meta folder in Drive) and replace the old FEB - AM and MAY - AM rows (more campaigns; ThruPlays and similar detail columns are kept only on unchanged rows); every month's campaign totals now equal Meta's account totals for spend, impressions and clicks.
- Page follows (added 2026-10-22 from Mich's Business Suite exports in the Meta Drive folder): `pulse.fb_page_metrics` in long format (metric follows_month = new follows per month, summed from the daily Follows.csv, before unfollows; followers_lifetime, net_follows and unfollows are whole-period figures from the Audience screenshot). `data._prepare` returns `S["follows"]` (monthly) and `S["page"]` (whole-period totals). They add two cards in `data.half_cards` (new Facebook follows, new follows per 1,000 of ad spend; need a follows figure for every month of both periods) and the matching rows on the First vs Second Half tab. Net new followers and follower growth per period are NOT computed: unfollows are only known for the whole period. The daily follows total (CSV) and what the screenshot implies differ by about 4% (unexplained); the internal view says so. The seed creates the table and the reader's read access itself (the older 21 reader script already lists it).
- Platform analytics figures (added 2026-10-23, supplied by the team, not computed here): Facebook followers, TikTok followers, TikTok reach and TikTok engagements of a WHOLE period, stored as `fb_followers`, `tt_followers`, `tt_reach`, `tt_engagement` rows of `pulse.fb_page_metrics` (one row per half, period_start and period_end set to the half). `data._prepare` returns them as `S["tt_platform"]`; `data.platform_period` returns the ones whose stored period equals exactly the two periods compared, so `half_cards` shows them (third Audience Growth row, no sparkline) only for those periods and the app shows nothing otherwise. They are not sums of posts or videos (TikTok reach is not TikTok video views, which stay as a separate card); their source and exact definition (period-end follower totals, TikTok reach as unique viewers) are NOT confirmed yet, so the internal view says so. The Facebook followers total differs slightly from Business Suite's lifetime followers, so the whole-period caption no longer prints the lifetime total when the supplied figure is present. "Facebook post views" is labelled "Facebook post views (impressions)" everywhere: it is Meta's Views metric of the page posts (every time a post was shown or played, repeats included, organic and boosted posts together), not a number of people and not purely organic; "Ad impressions" (Meta's account totals, campaign rows as fallback) is shown beside it. Also added: "Total reach" (sum of the monthly campaign reach, the figure the Ad Spend Report owner uses) and "Ad post engagements" (Ads Manager post engagements of the Facebook ads, the count behind engagements per ₱1,000).
- Rules: never commit `data/` or `secrets.toml`; only the read-only BPP login in the app; never ask for or print passwords; run with a venv built from requirements.txt (Streamlit 1.66; the shared ../.venv still has 1.38 for PediaWell) or Python 3.12; verify changes by running the app and checking September 2026 (spend 19,458.54, reach 1,014,541, cost per 1,000 reached 19.18).
- Client vs internal view: the sidebar has an "Internal view" switch (off by default). Off = what the client sees (no source line, no yellow markers, no Ads Manager vs Ad Spend Report data check). On = the team's data-quality view. It is a display switch, not security.
- Deck: `report_deck.py` builds the 8-slide Swarm-look monthly .pptx, and with `--halves` the 2-slide half vs half review titled "Platform Distribution Level Up" (the official title of the slide group), in the look of the Share-Led Content Focus deck (cream, dark ink, green/orange, DM Sans), in three layouts (`--layout cards` default, `halves` = first period left / next period right like the dashboard's first tab and the layout chosen for the client, `table`; with more than nine cards on a slide the halves layout switches to two columns with the pill beside the number), from `data.half_cards` (the KPI Cards tab's cards; see README_REPORT.md); `out/` is git-ignored. Needs `requirements-report.txt` (python-pptx). Google Slides template fill is not built (no template yet).
- Access password: optional `app_password` in Secrets (top level, above `[supabase]`) or APP_PASSWORD env var; when set the app shows a password form and loads nothing until it matches (`hmac.compare_digest`). Unset = open. Shared secret, no lockout, not real security.
- Open items: target audiences and weekly reach (not in the SSOT), sentiment tab, follower growth, brand health, an n8n loader to refresh the Supabase tables.
