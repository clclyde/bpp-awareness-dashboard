# BPP brand awareness dashboard: project context

Own project (private repo `clclyde/bpp-awareness-dashboard`, separate from the PediaWell dashboard). Streamlit 1.66.0 prototype for BPP (Budol-Proof Pinoy: Facebook page and TikTok), requested by Sir Joven.

- Design (editorial, redone 2026-10-20): the page opens with the finding as a headline sentence, then big figures with sparklines (native `st.metric`, no card boxes), then evidence. Colours are the Project Swarm deck palette (white, navy #073763, blue #0B3691, yellow #F1C232, red #D10000, text #3F3F3F); fonts Montserrat (body) and Source Serif 4 (headings), all set in `.streamlit/config.toml`. Custom HTML: a plain yellow rule at the top, a red bar at the bottom, and the KPI Cards tab (added 2026-10-21 at Sir Joven's request: the half vs half comparison as cards, read in his order: the number now, a filled change pill green = better / red = worse / grey = within 2%, then the previous number and the absolute change; key KPIs add a monthly sparkline with hover, first period grey and second blue; own period selector `kc_n` / `kc_start`, default 6 months from Oct 2025; groups ordered to make the renewal case, strongest first: Audience Growth, More From Every Peso Spent, Spend and Activity (every figure stays on the page, only the order changes); cards in a row have equal height; CTR and cost per click appear only when both periods have clicks, i.e. from Mar 2026 or after the monthly campaign reports are loaded). Tabs: First vs second half (default view, requested by Sir Joven 2026-10-20: two equal periods side by side with growth and cost, own selector of period length and start month, default 6 months per period, first period starting Oct 2025: Oct 2025 to Mar 2026 vs Apr to Sep 2026 (chosen 2026-10-21; the start month stays selectable); sections ordered strongest first for the renewal case (2026-10-21): Facebook Page and TikTok (reach per post, post views, TikTok views, posts, engagement rate), Cost Efficiency (cost per engagement first), Paid Awareness (reach, ad engagement rate, cost per 1,000 reached, spend, frequency); TikTok is only compared when its data covers the first period; no video view-through here, the exports have no ThruPlays before Mar 2026), Overview, Awareness trend, Content, Channel Diagnostics. Channel Diagnostics (added 2026-10-21, mirrors the PediaWell tab of the same name; two equal periods with its own selector, 6 months per period and the first period starting Oct 2025 by default, each channel on two adjacent rows: first period = Baseline, second period = status vs the first): paid channels table (Facebook ads full metrics, TikTok ads spend only), organic channels table (Facebook page, TikTok), Facebook ads by campaign type. Status = change in cost per mille (paid) or engagement rate / engagement per view (organic) from the first period to the second, with the PediaWell thresholds (Improved at 5% better or more, Steady within 5%, Slightly weaker up to 15% worse, Weaker beyond; proposed, to be calibrated). A campaign type with under 2% of the spend gets no status. TikTok ad spend (`ref_tt`: snapshot key `tiktok_ad_spend`, Supabase `pulse.ad_spend_monthly` client_code BPP-TT, source_tab 'SSOT TT tabs' for Aug and Oct 2025, 'Ad Spend Report 2025' for Sep, Nov and Dec 2025 (Sep: the SEPT - TT tab has boost rows without a video that the billing does not include), 'Campaign Spend Monitoring' for 2026) is shown only when every month of a period has a figure. The first tab has a Cost Efficiency section (added 2026-10-21 at Sir Joven's request: unit costs should go down and what each PHP 1,000 buys should go up): cost per mille, per 1,000 reached, per engagement, plus per click and per ThruPlay only when both periods are from Mar 2026 onward (earlier exports have no clicks or ThruPlays), and a by-campaign-type table, because the blended costs also move with the campaign mix (`data.unit_costs`, `data.unit_costs_by_objective`). Follows Streamlit's own design guidance: theme in config.toml, native metrics/dataframes, no CSS unless asked. Needs Streamlit 1.66 (bordered/sparkline metrics, `delta_description`, theme fonts and chart palettes).
- Data: Supabase `pulse.social_ads_monthly`, `fb_posts`, `tiktok_videos` and BPP rows of `ad_spend_monthly`, read with the read-only `pulse_reader_bpp` login (secrets or `PULSE_DB_*` env vars). Local fallback: git-ignored `data/bpp_snapshot.json`. Source of the data: BPP Content SSOT sheet (Ads Manager exports, Facebook post tabs + YTD Meta, YTD TikTok + the 2025 TT tabs). Refresh is manual: rebuild the snapshot, run `supabase/loaders/gen_22_bpp_seed.py` (parent project, git-ignored), run the seed in Supabase.
- Definitions follow the deck: reach is the sum of campaign reach rows (overstates unique reach), monthly not weekly, no target audience yet (card pending); frequency = impressions / reach; cost per 1,000 reached = spend / reach x 1,000 (reach campaigns only in comparisons); engagement rate = (reactions + comments + shares) / reach; TikTok has views only. Aug and Sep 2025 figures in the SSOT "Tables" tab are 2025, not targets.
- TikTok has no videos in the SSOT for Oct to Dec 2025: those posts were added from TikTok Studio Posts-list screenshots (lifetime views/likes/comments, no shares, no links; a list value shown as "30K" is rounded). Any month that still has no TikTok rows is treated as missing data, not zero: the comparison tab shows the partial total labelled "(partial)" with the months it covers, and no change percentage. The explanatory notes about the missing months (comparison tab and Overview) are internal-view only; the client sees just the "(partial)" label and the months it covers.
- Data fixes 2026-10-21: AUG - AM contains its export twice (second block column-shifted) and OCT - AM row 27 repeats row 7; both duplicates are removed from the snapshot/seed (215 ad rows). Facebook post tabs include boosted posts (most views are boosted), so never label them organic. The Ad Spend Report's BPP-TT line mixes ex-VAT and VAT-inclusive amounts; the Campaign Spend Monitoring sheet has the consistent TikTok spend for 2026; Nov and Dec 2025 TikTok spend come from the Summary 2025 tab of the Ad Spend Report 2025 (source_tab 'Ad Spend Report 2025'). Full audit: parent project supabase/BPP_SOURCE_AUDIT.md.
- Known data gaps: Feb and May 2026 Ads Manager exports are incomplete (spend below the Ad Spend Report; shown yellow on the trend charts). Apr 2026 is complete: the Ad Spend Report's April figure is VAT-inclusive, so the app uses the ex-VAT amount from Campaign Spend Monitoring instead (snapshot key `ad_spend_fb_csm`, Supabase `ad_spend_monthly` BPP-FB rows with source_tab 'Campaign Spend Monitoring'); March 2026 is exported per ad; the JUL - Meta tab header is broken (handled).
- Rules: never commit `data/` or `secrets.toml`; only the read-only BPP login in the app; never ask for or print passwords; run with a venv built from requirements.txt (Streamlit 1.66; the shared ../.venv still has 1.38 for PediaWell) or Python 3.12; verify changes by running the app and checking September 2026 (spend 19,458.54, reach 1,014,541, cost per 1,000 reached 19.18).
- Client vs internal view: the sidebar has an "Internal view" switch (off by default). Off = what the client sees (no source line, no yellow markers, no Ads Manager vs Ad Spend Report data check). On = the team's data-quality view. It is a display switch, not security. Real fix: re-export Feb and May 2026 in the SSOT.
- Deck: `report_deck.py` builds the 8-slide Swarm-look .pptx (see README_REPORT.md); `out/` is git-ignored. Needs `requirements-report.txt` (python-pptx). Google Slides template fill is not built (no template yet).
- Access password: optional `app_password` in Secrets (top level, above `[supabase]`) or APP_PASSWORD env var; when set the app shows a password form and loads nothing until it matches (`hmac.compare_digest`). Unset = open. Shared secret, no lockout, not real security.
- Open items: target audiences and weekly reach (not in the SSOT), sentiment tab, follower growth, brand health, an n8n loader to refresh the Supabase tables.
